# Why are logic gates so special?

Logic gates, in various forms, are very important and special as they are essentially the building blocks of all digital electronic systems. 
A logic gate can receive one or more signals and produce an output based on a set of logical rules. 
Although each individual logic gate can only perform very simple tasks, when you have million or even billion of them, they can be combined to perform complex operations 
such as calculations. Modern computers, phones, calculators and even many home appliances rely on logic gates to process information.

What makes these logic gates especially useful is that they operate in a base 2 system, which is represented in 1's or 0's and is called binary
This allows for very accurate hardware as the system does not need to try guess between many voltage levels,
logic gates can also be connected in almost any way imaginable, which makes them very flexible. By arranging these gates in different patterns, they can be designed into circuits that perform specific tasks without needing an entire computer dedicated to a simple task.

Another reason logic gates are special is there speed. Electronic signals travel quickly though a circuit which allows these logic gates to perform millions if not billions of operations per second. This high speed makes digital technology very suitable for real time applications. 

## Use cases of logic gates

Logic gates are used in many areas of technology. In computers they form part of the processor that carries out arithmetic operations and processing of data. Memory devices, such as RAM and some ROM also use logic gates to store information. 

Logic gates are widely used in control systems, from automatic doors to washing machines and microwaves. These systems use digital circuits containing logic gates to make decisions based on sensor inputs. Industrial machines use them to monitor conditions and control motors and or actuators
